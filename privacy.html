Koora Live needs no account and collects no personal data. This page is the full picture.

On your device only

Language, theme, favorites, kickoff reminders, recently seen matches and your ad preference live in your own browser storage. They never leave your device — there is no user database.

Advertising

Pages show banner ads from third-party networks, which may use cookies to count views. You can switch ads off anytime with the Ads button at the bottom of the screen.

Video streams

Video plays from third-party stream hosts inside a sandboxed player. Those hosts apply their own policies.

Children

Koora Live is a general-audience site. With no accounts, there is nothing knowingly collected from anyone, children included.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions? Reach us on Telegram.
