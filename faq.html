How do I watch a match?

Pick any match on the homepage to open its watch page, then tap ▶ to start the stream.

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, everything is free and there is no account, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — phone, tablet or desktop, in any modern browser.

The stream stutters. What now?

Pick the next server from the Stream sources list — a different host usually fixes it. You can also report a dead server with the ⚠ button.

Can I get English commentary?

Yes. With the interface in English, VIPBox servers with English commentary play first. Other servers are Arabic.

Why does the minute keep updating?

The minute fuses the live feed with kickoff times and refreshes automatically.

Will the 2026 World Cup be covered?

Yes — fixtures, live scores and streams appear here with kickoff times.

How do I report a problem?

Use the ⚠ Report button on the watch page for dead servers, or reach us on Telegram — see Contact.
