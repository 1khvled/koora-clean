What is Koora Live?

Koora Live is a free football companion: every match day you get fixtures with kickoff times, live scores with minutes that update on their own, results, and clean HD streams. No account, no popups.

Features

  • Live scores and minutes that keep updating by themselves
  • English commentary on VIPBox servers, Arabic on the rest
  • Favorites, kickoff reminders and match pages that load fast
  • English, French and Arabic interface with dark mode
  • Fully mobile-friendly — phone, tablet or desktop

Competitions

Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Saudi Pro League, UEFA Champions League — and the FIFA World Cup 2026.

Good to know

Streams play from third-party hosts inside a sandboxed player, so availability can vary from match to match. If a server stutters, pick the next one from the list.
